Sally Vivian Shepherd

I am currently an undergraduate—class of 2027—studying Astronomy and Physics at Mount Holyoke College on South Hadley, MA. I've lergely studied and researched stars, particularly stellar binaries, and I have a specific interest in X-ray binaries due to their extreme nature, as well as how object interactions impact both stellar and neutron star evolution.

Visual Orbits of Spectroscoptic Binaries

My first undergraduate research experience where I worked under Professor Katie Lester at Mount Holyoke College. I worked with interferometry data to characterize the orbit of a spectroscopic binary. I presented this research in a poster at the 247th AAS meeting in Phoenix, Arizona. Paper to-be-published.

  • See iPoster

Bioessential Budgets of Planetary Hosts

My summer 2026 REU research at the University of New Mexico. I worked under Dr. David R. Coria and Professor Diana Dragomir at the University of New Mexico. I used stellar spectra to calculate CNOPS abundances. I plan to present my research in a poster at the 249th AAS meeting in Salt Lake City, Utah.
